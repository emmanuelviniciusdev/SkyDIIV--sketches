# Colors

The brand palette is faded and warm. Do not use pure black or pure white. Borders use stone warm at reduced opacity.

Default interface text uses a deeper slate gray and stone warm so type holds against the page.

Compact filled actions and chips use their accessible fills with on-primary text.
