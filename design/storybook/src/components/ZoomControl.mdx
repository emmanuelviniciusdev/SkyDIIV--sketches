# ZoomControl

A bordered bar with minus, a range, and plus. The row matches Input height and uses the card fill with a stone border. The filled range is accessible primary. The thumb is a small rounded square on the card fill, not a circle.

Pair with Label through `aria-labelledby`. The range is a custom control, so `htmlFor` does not name it. Hint copy stays outside the control.

The slider needs `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`. Icon-only steppers always take `decrementAriaLabel` and `incrementAriaLabel`.
